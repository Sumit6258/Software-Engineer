# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people learn. Everyone taking part, whether they are
reading, asking questions, reporting an error or contributing a lesson, is
expected to help keep it a respectful and welcoming place.

## Our standard

- Be kind and assume good faith. Many contributors are learning the subject
  they are writing about.
- Criticise the work, not the person. A correction should say what is wrong, why,
  and ideally what a better version looks like.
- Welcome beginners. "Why is this obvious?" is a sign the lesson needs work, not
  that the asker does.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work. Do not copy text from other sources into lessons.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Deliberately misleading technical content, including fabricated citations.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or pushing a change after the maintainers
  have explained why it will not be accepted.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict
or permanently ban participants who violate this code. Reports should go to the
maintainers' conduct contact, which is **[to be set by the repository owner]**.
Until it is published, open an issue asking for a private channel without
including details of the incident.

*The repository owner may replace this text with an established code such as the
Contributor Covenant; if so, keep the enforcement contact above up to date.*
