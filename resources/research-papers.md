# Research Papers and Historical Documents

Foundational papers cited by the lessons. Entries give bibliographic details rather than links, because stable links to some of these change over time; use the venue and title to find an authoritative copy. The list grows with the library.

| Reference | Relevance |
|---|---|
| A. M. Turing, "On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem", *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society*, series 2, vol. 42, 1936–37 | Defines the abstract machine that underlies the idea of a general-purpose, programmable computer. |
| J. von Neumann, "First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC", 1945 | The stored-program architecture in which instructions and data share one memory. |
| C. E. Shannon, "A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits", *Transactions of the American Institute of Electrical Engineers*, vol. 57, 1938 | Shows that Boolean algebra describes switching circuits, the basis of digital logic. |
| C. E. Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication", *Bell System Technical Journal*, vol. 27, 1948 | Introduces the bit as the unit of information. |
| D. Goldberg, "What Every Computer Scientist Should Know About Floating-Point Arithmetic", *ACM Computing Surveys*, vol. 23, no. 1, 1991 | The classic explanation of rounding error and IEEE 754. |
| W. Kahan, "Further Remarks on Reducing Truncation Errors", *Communications of the ACM*, vol. 8, no. 1, 1965 | Compensated (Kahan) summation. |
| D. Cohen, "On Holy Wars and a Plea for Peace", *IEEE Computer*, vol. 14, no. 10, 1981 | Names and analyses the big-endian versus little-endian dispute. |
