# Recommended Books

A curated, growing list. Each entry is a real, published book; editions are given only where I am certain of them. Entries are grouped by the subject they support and will expand as the library does. "Level" is a rough guide.

## Part I — Computer fundamentals, data representation and architecture

| Book | Why it helps | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Charles Petzold, *Code: The Hidden Language of Computer Hardware and Software* (Microsoft Press, 2nd edition, 2022) | A narrative path from simple switches and relays up to a working processor. The best first read for the whole of Subject 1. | Beginner |
| Noam Nisan and Shimon Schocken, *The Elements of Computing Systems: Building a Modern Computer from First Principles* (MIT Press, 2nd edition, 2021) | You build a computer from logic gates upward; ideal companion to Subjects 1, 3 and 10. | Beginner to intermediate |
| David A. Patterson and John L. Hennessy, *Computer Organization and Design: The Hardware/Software Interface* (Morgan Kaufmann; RISC-V edition) | The standard text on how instruction sets, datapaths, pipelines and caches work. | Intermediate |
| Randal E. Bryant and David R. O'Hallaron, *Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective* (Pearson, 3rd edition) | Machine-level representation of integers, floats and programs, from a programmer's viewpoint. | Intermediate |
| Andrew S. Tanenbaum and Todd Austin, *Structured Computer Organization* (Pearson) | A layered tour from digital logic to operating-system level. | Intermediate |
| Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 2: Seminumerical Algorithms* (Addison-Wesley, 3rd edition) | Rigorous treatment of positional number systems and floating-point arithmetic. | Advanced |
| Michael L. Overton, *Numerical Computing with IEEE Floating Point Arithmetic* (SIAM, 2001) | A short, clear book on IEEE 754 and its consequences. | Intermediate |
| Jean-Michel Muller and others, *Handbook of Floating-Point Arithmetic* (Birkhäuser, 2nd edition, 2018) | The comprehensive reference on floating-point formats and algorithms. | Advanced |
| Henry S. Warren Jr., *Hacker's Delight* (Addison-Wesley, 2nd edition, 2012) | Bit manipulation, integer arithmetic tricks and two's-complement idioms. | Intermediate to advanced |
| Shu Lin and Daniel J. Costello Jr., *Error Control Coding* (Pearson) | The standard textbook on error-detecting and error-correcting codes. | Advanced |
| David Salomon, *Data Compression: The Complete Reference* (Springer) | Lossless and lossy compression, from Huffman coding to image and video codecs. | Intermediate to advanced |
| Alan V. Oppenheim and Ronald W. Schafer, *Discrete-Time Signal Processing* (Pearson) | Sampling, aliasing and digital signals. | Advanced |
| Martin Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* (O'Reilly) | The chapter on encoding and evolution covers serialization formats and compatibility. | Intermediate |
