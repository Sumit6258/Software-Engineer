## Introduction

Distributed ledgers, consensus, cryptographic foundations, smart contracts, decentralised applications and their security and limits.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Explain how a blockchain reaches agreement and what it guarantees
- Write and test a simple smart contract
- Recognise common smart-contract vulnerabilities
