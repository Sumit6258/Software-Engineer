## Introduction

Sequential decision making: Markov decision processes, value and policy methods, deep reinforcement learning and applications.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Formulate a problem as an MDP
- Implement tabular Q-learning
- Explain policy-gradient methods and their instabilities
