## Introduction

Fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, security of AI systems, governance frameworks and regulation.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Identify and measure bias and other harms
- Apply a risk-management framework to an AI system
- Document a model's intended use and limits
