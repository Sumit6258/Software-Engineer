## Introduction

What AI is and is not: its history, problem formulations, search, knowledge representation, learning and the landscape of approaches, before any machine-learning or LLM specifics.

> Start with understanding what AI actually is before jumping into LLMs.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Distinguish symbolic, statistical and learning-based approaches
- Formulate a problem as search or optimisation
- Describe the AI landscape accurately, including its limits
