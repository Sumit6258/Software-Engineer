## Introduction

The economics of engineering decisions: cost, value, technical debt, build-versus-buy and cloud cost management.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Quantify the cost of technical debt
- Make and justify build-versus-buy decisions
- Estimate the total cost of ownership
