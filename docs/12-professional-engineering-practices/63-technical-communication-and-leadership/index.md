## Introduction

Writing, presenting and documenting technical work, collaborating, mentoring and leading engineering teams.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Write clear design documents and decision records
- Give and receive code review feedback
- Lead technical discussions towards decisions

## Prerequisites

None. This subject can be started without knowledge of another subject in this library.
