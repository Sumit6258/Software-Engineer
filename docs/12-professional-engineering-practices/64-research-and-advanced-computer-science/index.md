## Introduction

Computability, complexity, formal methods, type theory, advanced algorithms and the research frontier in systems, languages and AI.

> These subjects are particularly relevant for research engineers, systems researchers, compiler engineers, AI researchers and academics.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Reason about computability and complexity classes
- Read and critique a research paper
- Apply formal specification to a small system
