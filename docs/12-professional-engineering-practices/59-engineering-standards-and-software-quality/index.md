## Introduction

Standards, quality models, process improvement, compliance and metrics for software engineering organisations.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Describe the major software standards and their purpose
- Select meaningful quality metrics
- Prepare for an audit or assessment
