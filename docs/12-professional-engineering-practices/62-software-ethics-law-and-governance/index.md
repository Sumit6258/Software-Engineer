## Introduction

Professional ethics, intellectual property, licensing, privacy and data-protection law, accessibility obligations and organisational governance.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this subject you should be able to:

- Reason through ethical dilemmas using professional codes
- Choose and comply with open-source licences
- Recognise privacy and compliance obligations

## Prerequisites

None. This subject can be started without knowledge of another subject in this library.
